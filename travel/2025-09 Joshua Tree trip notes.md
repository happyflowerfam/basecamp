# Joshua Tree — trip notes

- **Keys Ranch** — only accessible through Ranger events, requires advance reservation.
- **Hidden Valley trail** — fun.
- **Arch Rock** — good; lots of easy rocks to climb in the area.
- **Split Rock Trail + Face Rock Wash + Skull Rock via Discovery** — combined into one loop. Evaluated energy after each section; Samā could actually do it. Terrain was varied and fun.
- **Cottonwood Spring** — ranger talk about indigenous use of the space was very interesting. The drive there is amazing — you cross from the Mojave desert to the Colorado desert.
- **Keys View** — amazing viewpoint overlooking the Coachella Valley and the San Andreas Fault.
- **Cholla Cactus Garden** — closed during our visit but drove through; quite unique. Ocotillo patch nearby (flowers may be seasonal).

**NPS app** — quite useful. Download the park-specific portion before heading in; connectivity can be spotty. Also covers the geological features: volcanic Malapai Hill, and the mix of sedimentary, igneous, and metamorphic rocks in the same region.
