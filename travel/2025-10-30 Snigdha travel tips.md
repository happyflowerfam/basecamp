# Snigdha's travel tips

Kuai / Hawaii - notes from Snigdha
- Stay on Hanalai Bay side, Princeville
- The Princeville Hotel - too expensive for stay but do a breakfast bay because the view from that hotel is really good
- Small island - 1 hour
- Hikes starts at Ke'e Beach and ends at a different beach, jump into the water at the beach after hike
- Minimum 3 nights
- Helicopter tour
- Napali cliffs (by boat or helipcopter)
- Maui had those forest fires so we don't know how much it has recovered
- You could go to Oahu, Turtle Bay - take a flight to Kuai
- Leave Big Island - because it is big - Volcanoes National Park - but this is only cool if there is an actual volcano exploding
- When you drive from one side to the other side the view is beautiful, just take a long time to stop and go and take in the views - but you could skip this.
- Big Island - go to the eastern side of the island and see the volcano
- Maui and Oahu are better connected ... try to look for flights that end up at the other end of the island.
- Big Island cocktail bar called hidden nene - in city called Hilo
- Waipio valley - very cool - very beautiful
- 4 days in Hilo including 2 days in Volcanoes, 3 days in Kuai (calm better beaches, reef protected beaches)
1 week or 9 days
