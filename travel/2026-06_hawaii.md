## ✈️ Recommended Routing

**SFO → Kauai (LIH) → Big Island/Kona (KOA) ~~ scenic drive ~~ Hilo → KOA → SFO**

Start on Kauai, fly into Kona (cheaper than Hilo), spend night 1 on the Big Island near Waimea, then drive north through Waipio Valley to Hilo the next morning. Return home from KOA at the end.

- **Outbound:** SFO → Lihue, Kauai (LIH) — departs 9:00am PDT, arrives 11:50am HST (~5h50m)
- **Mid-trip:** Lihue (LIH) → Kona (KOA) — departs 5:00pm HST, arrives 5:40pm HST (~40 min)
- **Night 1 Big Island:** Drive straight from KOA to Hilo (~2.5 hrs) — arrive ~9pm, check in, sleep
- **Scenic drive (return day):** Hilo → **Waipio Valley lookout** → Waimea → KOA — done on June 13 afternoon on the way to the airport; leave Hilo by ~3:30pm to hit Waipio in good afternoon light (~5pm) and reach KOA by ~8pm
- **Return:** Kona (KOA) → SFO — departs 10:50pm HST, arrives 7:00am PDT (~5h10m)

> 💡 KOA flights are cheaper than ITO because Kona is the resort hub with more airline competition. Both arriving and departing from KOA means no one-way car rental drop fee — a bonus.

## 🌿 Kauai (4 days) — Hanalei/Princeville Area

### Where to Stay
Stay on the **north shore**: Princeville or Hanalei Bay area. Calmer, more beautiful, closest to the best hikes and beaches.

### Top Activities

**Beaches & Water**
- **Hanalei Bay** — wide, beautiful, calm; the iconic Kauai beach
- **Ke'e Beach** — stunning, good snorkeling, start of the Na Pali trail; calm reef-protected water
- **Lydgate Beach Park** — man-made snorkel pools, huge playground; great for a 6-year-old
- **Poipu Beach** (south shore) — shallowest, most family-friendly, sea turtles

**Hikes**
- **Hanakapi'ai Trail from Ke'e Beach** — first 2 miles is kid-friendly; dramatic cliffs, lush jungle; *don't go past the 2-mile mark with a child*

**Na Pali Coast**
- **By boat** — 90-min–4 hr snorkel/sightseeing tour; highly recommended; kids love it
- **By helicopter** — spectacular aerial views of the cliffs; pricey but unforgettable (~$200–$300/person)
- Snigdha's rec: *boat or helicopter — do at least one*

**Other**
- **Breakfast with a view at Princeville Hotel** — Snigdha's rec; the hotel is expensive but a breakfast there is worth it for the bay view
- **Limahuli Garden** — botanical garden in a valley; good for an easy morning walk
- **Waimea Canyon** (south) — "Grand Canyon of the Pacific"; easy lookout drives + short trails

### Gluten-Free Picks (Kauai)
- **Tiki Tacos** — dedicated GF taco shop
- **Papaya's Natural Foods & Café** (Kapa'a) — extensive GF menu, GF pizza
- **Bar Acuda** — upscale Mediterranean/Hawaiian, dedicated GF menu (treat night)
- **Kōloa Fish Market** — traditional Hawaiian poke bowls with GF soy sauce
- **Hanalei area food trucks** — many naturally GF (poke, grilled fish, rice bowls)
- **Hawaiian cuisine in general** — largely rice-based, naturally GF-friendly

## 🌋 Big Island (4 days) — Hilo Side

### Where to Stay
**All 4 nights (June 9–12):** Stay in or near **Hilo** (east side) — most affordable on the Big Island, best base for volcanoes, waterfalls, and Hilo town. You'll drive straight from KOA on arrival night (~9pm), and do the scenic Waipio Valley drive on the way back to KOA on June 13.

### Top Activities

**Volcanoes** *(2 days)*
- **Hawaii Volcanoes National Park** — entry ~$35/car; worth every penny
  - **Chain of Craters Road** — scenic drive to lava fields at the ocean; dramatic
  - **Thurston Lava Tube** — underground walk through a lava tube; kids love it
  - **Kīlauea Caldera** — viewpoint of the active crater
  - **Junior Ranger Program** — free; your 6-year-old gets a badge; highly recommended
- Check live volcano activity before trip — if lava is flowing it's even more spectacular

**Waterfalls & Nature**
- **Rainbow Falls** — 5 min walk from parking; gorgeous rainbow in the morning mist
- **Akaka Falls State Park** — easy paved 0.4-mile loop; two dramatic waterfalls; 10 miles from Hilo
- **Waipio Valley Lookout** — one of the most stunning views in all of Hawaii; dramatic valley cliffs; *Snigdha called it "very cool, very beautiful"*

**Beaches**
- **Punalu'u Black Sand Beach** — unique volcanic black sand; sea turtles often rest here; don't miss

**Hilo Town**
- **Hilo Farmers Market** (Wed & Sat) — local produce, tropical fruit, GF snacks, souvenirs; great morning outing
- **Hidden Nene** — cocktail bar in Hilo; Snigdha's rec for adults (evening out)

### Gluten-Free Tips (Big Island)
- Hilo Farmers Market has naturally GF fresh produce, poke, and local food
- Stock up at **Costco in Hilo** for groceries — much cheaper than resort areas
- Fewer dedicated GF restaurants than Kauai — plan ahead
- Poke bowls (ask for GF tamari instead of soy sauce), grilled fish, fresh fruit all safe

## 🎒 Logistics & Tips

- **Rental car**: Essential on both islands; book in advance; June is busy. On the Big Island, pick up and drop off at **KOA** (both flights use Kona) — no one-way drop fee needed
- **Sun protection**: Hawaii sun is intense; reef-safe sunscreen required at many beaches
- **Rain on north shore Kauai**: It rains — bring a light layer and embrace it; everything is green because of it
- **Kid packing**: Water shoes for lava fields and rocky beaches; snorkel set (cheaper to bring than rent)
- **Gluten tips**: Call restaurants ahead; Hawaiian food is naturally GF-friendly
	- **Drive times**: Both islands are small enough to day-trip across; Kauai is ~1 hr coast to coast
