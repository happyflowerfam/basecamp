### Top Activities

**Beaches & Water**
- **Hanalei Bay** (~25 min) — wide, beautiful, calm; the iconic Kauai beach
- **Ke'e Beach** (~40 min) — stunning, good snorkeling, start of the Na Pali trail; calm reef-protected water
- **Lydgate Beach Park** (~10 min) — man-made snorkel pools, huge playground; great for a 6-year-old
- **Poipu Beach** (~35 min, south shore) — shallowest, most family-friendly, sea turtles

**Hikes**
- **Hanakapi'ai Trail from Ke'e Beach** — first 2 miles is kid-friendly; dramatic cliffs, lush jungle; *don't go past the 2-mile mark with a child*

**Na Pali Coast — Makana Charters, Na Pali Kai III** *(booked: Jun 7, departs 2pm from Kikiaola Harbor)*
- Departs Kikiaola Harbor · rugged cliffs → Polihale shoreline → Kalalau Valley waterfalls → sea caves → snorkel + lunch
- [Directions to Kikiaola Small Boat Harbor](https://makanacharters.com/kekaha-directions/)
- **Sea caves:** Pirates Cave (Waiahuakua), Rainbow Falls cave, Honopu Archway *(King Kong, Pirates of the Caribbean, James Bond filming location)*
- **Snorkel spots:** Makole, Ke'e Beach tunnels, Nualolo Kai, Kekaha/Kaumakani (conditions permitting) · ~1 hr snorkeling near end of trip
- **Wildlife:** Spinner + Bottlenose dolphins, coral reefs, fish, Hawaiian green sea turtles
- **Included:** Snorkel gear + flotation devices · deli lunch (Turkey/Ham/Roast Beef/Vegetarian) + snacks + local juices + soft drinks · BYOB alcohol (after snorkeling only)
- **Wear:** Swimwear; bring hat, towel, sunscreen · can bring own snorkel gear or use theirs

**Other**

- **Limahuli Garden** — botanical garden in a valley; good for an easy morning walk
- **Waimea Canyon** (~50 min, south) — "Grand Canyon of the Pacific"; easy lookout drives + short trails

### Gluten-Free Picks (Kauai)

- Hilo Farmers Market has naturally GF fresh produce, poke, and local food
- Stock up at **Costco in Hilo** for groceries — much cheaper than resort areas
- Fewer dedicated GF restaurants than Kauai — plan ahead
- Poke bowls (ask for GF tamari), grilled fish, fresh fruit all safe

## 🎒 Logistics & Tips

- **Sun protection**: Hawaii sun is intense; reef-safe sunscreen required at many beaches
- **Rain on north shore Kauai**: It rains — bring a light layer and embrace it; everything is green because of it
- **Kid packing**: Water shoes for lava fields and rocky beaches; snorkel set (cheaper to bring than rent); kids' swim vest from home
- **Rip currents**: Real hazard on Hawaii beaches. North shore Kauai (Hanalei, Ke'e) and Punalu'u black sand beach (Big Island) can be dangerous — look-don't-swim unless conditions are clearly calm. **Lydgate Beach Park** (Kauai) has enclosed man-made snorkel pools — safest option for Samā. Always check conditions before entering open water.
- **Gluten tips**: Call restaurants ahead; Hawaiian food is naturally GF-friendly
- **Drive times**: Both islands are small enough to day-trip; Kauai is ~1 hr coast to coast. Punalu'u is on the same Hwy 11 route as Volcanoes — easy to combine on the Volcanoes day.
