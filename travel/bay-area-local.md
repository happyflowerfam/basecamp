# Bay Area Local Gems

## Allied Arts Guild — Menlo Park
75 Arbor Rd, Menlo Park
Free to visit. Lush secret garden, cafe for brunch, artist workshops.
Mon–Sat 10am–5pm, closed Sunday.

## Montalvo Arts Center — Saratoga
15400 Montalvo Rd, Saratoga, CA
Mediterranean villa, gardens, forest trails, art installations. Free to visit.

## Pulgas Water Temple — Redwood City
56 Cañada Rd, Redwood City, CA 94062
Free, open Mon–Fri 9–5. Lavender in bloom June & July. Greek architecture. Great for dates/picnics/photos. ~30 min from SF.
