## Don't Forget Before You Leave Home

### Final Prep
- **Check weather forecast** (adjust clothing accordingly)
- **Confirm all reservations** (Washington Monument, Air & Space Museum, etc.)
- **Download offline maps** (in case of poor cell service)
- **Research backup indoor activities** (for rain or excessive heat)
- **Print important phone numbers** (hotels, tour companies, etc.)
### Last-Minute Packing Tips
- **Pack one day's worth of clothes in carry-on** (in case checked luggage is delayed)
- **Bring empty water bottles** (fill after security if flying)
- **Pack heavier items in checked luggage** (to make walking easier)
- **Leave room in suitcase** (for souvenirs from all those museum gift shops!)
## Weather Considerations
- **July D.C. Weather**: Hot and humid (80-90°F days, 70°F nights)
- **July NYC Weather**: Hot and humid (80-85°F days, 70-75°F nights) but with more concrete heat retention
- **Possible afternoon thunderstorms** in both cities
- **High humidity** in both locations - plan for lots of sweating and hydration needs
- **NYC Urban Heat Island Effect**: Concrete and asphalt make it feel 5-10°F hotter than actual temperature
## Pro Tips for D.C. with Kids
- **Arrive right at museum opening** (10 AM for most museums)
- **Plan rest breaks** (every 45-60 minutes)
- **Take photos of exhibits** (for discussing later when child is more interested)
- **Start walking the Mall early** (before heat builds up)
- **Plan for shade breaks** (limited shade between monuments)
- **Bring more water than you think you need**
- **Consider ride-share** between distant attractions
### NYC-Specific Tips
- **Master the subway system** (download offline maps, practice with stroller)
- **Plan for walking** (even with subway, lots of walking to/from stations)
- **Embrace food trucks and street vendors** (fun, fast, usually kid-friendly)
- **Book Broadway shows in advance** (especially kid-friendly matinees)
- **Plan indoor backup activities** (for excessive heat days)
- **Carry cash** (many small vendors, tips, emergencies)
- **Stay hydrated** (urban heat is intense, dehydration happens quickly)
