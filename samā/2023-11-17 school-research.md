# School Research — Tali Krieger (Nov 17, 2023)

Tali taught Diana's youngest daughter in 3rd grade at Kids Connection. Then Brewer Island 4th grade, then 1 year at Wornick Jewish Day School. Now does parent coaching (back issues).

## Schools

**Brewer Island / Foster City public** — max 20 kids/class. Quality depends heavily on the teacher (element of luck — find out who the teacher will be). Kids Connection afterschool program: advanced classes in math and reading level; freedom to bring in curriculum.

**Wornick Jewish Day School** — great school, Jewish values. Up to 20% non-Jewish. Jewish studies + holidays (many). Class sizes small, teachers warm and nurturing. Change of administration ~2 years ago felt in upper grades (5th). Tour + shadow option: half-day in school. More bureaucratic than public.

**Montessori** — pretty permanent fit (free spirit, doesn't like being told what to do).

## Key insight
Differentiation should start in 4th grade. At the end of the day, it depends on the teacher.
