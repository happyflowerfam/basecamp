# Camp Keff Policies 2025

## Payment
- $200 deposit per week due at registration
- Remaining tuition: 2 payments — March 13, 2025 and May 16, 2025 (final)
- Registrations after May 16 require full payment upfront
- Unpaid balances → waitlist on May 19, 2025

## Refunds
- $100 per week is non-refundable
- **No refunds after May 9, 2025**
- All refund requests in writing to camps@pjcc.org by May 9
- No proration for absence, illness, or withdrawal during camp

## Changes
- $25 admin fee per change; subject to space availability

camps@pjcc.org | campkeff.org
