# SMFCSD Enrollment — Required Documents

1. **Proof of age** — certified birth certificate, passport, baptismal certificate, registrar statement, or parent affidavit if none available
2. **Immunization record** — original, up-to-date
3. **Health exam report** (encouraged at Kindergarten, required by 1st grade)
4. **Parent/guardian photo ID** — CA driver's license, CA state ID, passport, consulate ID, or other photo ID
5. **Proof of district residency** (one of the following, dated within 60 days):
   - Declaration of District Residency
   - Current property tax bill
   - Rental/lease agreement (listing parent/guardian name, child name, property manager)
   - Homeowner's or renter's insurance policy
   - State/federal tax return with W-2 (remove SSN and income info)
   - Payroll/check stub with name and address
   - Voter registration
   - Current utility bill (PG&E, landline, water, garbage, cable TV)
   - Government agency correspondence

New residents: 30 days after enrollment to provide utility bill.
