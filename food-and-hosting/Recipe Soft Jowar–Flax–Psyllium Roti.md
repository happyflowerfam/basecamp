
# **🫓 Soft Jowar–Flax–Psyllium Roti**
## **Weekly Batch Cooking Guide**

### **Makes: 24 rotis (4 days × 2 meals × 3 rotis, or adjust as you like)**

---
## **🧾 Ingredients (scaled accurately)**
* **Jowar flour** – **4 cups** (≈ 480 g)
* **Flaxseed powder** – **8 tbsp** (≈ 40 g)
* **Psyllium husk (isabgol)** – **8 tsp** (≈ 24 g)
* **Salt** – **2 tsp**
* **Oil or ghee** – **8 tsp** (≈ 40 ml)
* **Hot water** – **\~3 cups** (720–780 ml)
**Optional (highly recommended for softness)**
* **Curd (yogurt)** – **4 tbsp**

---

## **🧠 Batch-cooking strategy (important)**
👉 **Make dough once, cook in 2 rounds**
* Dough handles best within **60–90 minutes**
* Don’t leave uncooked dough overnight

---
## **🔥 Step-by-step batch method**

### **1️⃣ Mix dry ingredients**
In a large bowl:
* Jowar flour
* Flax powder
* Psyllium husk
* Salt
Mix very thoroughly.
### **2️⃣ Add oil/ghee**
Add **8 tsp oil/ghee** and rub into the flour.
### **3️⃣ Add HOT water**
* Use freshly boiled water cooled **30–40 seconds**
* Add gradually while mixing with a spatula
Dough should be **soft and sticky at first**.
### **4️⃣ First knead**
* Knead lightly once cool enough to touch
* Add curd (if using)
* Aim for **soft, slightly tacky dough**

### **5️⃣ Rest (non-negotiable)**
Cover and rest **10–12 minutes**.
### **6️⃣ Second knead**
* Knead **2–3 minutes**
* Dough should now be **smooth, elastic, crack-free**
If cracking → sprinkle hot water and knead again.
## **🧺 Portioning (do this once)**
* Divide into **24 equal balls**
* Lightly oil palms
* Roll balls smooth
* Keep covered with damp cloth
## **🍳 Cooking for the freezer (best texture)**

### **7️⃣ Roll**
* Roll between plastic/parchment
* 6–7 inch diameter
* No dry flour
### **8️⃣ Par-cook ONLY**
* Hot tawa, medium heat
* Cook until **raw look disappears**
* **NO brown spots**
* Flip once
👉 This is key for freezing softness.

### **9️⃣ Cool completely**
* Lay flat, single layer
* Do not stack while warm
## **🧊 Freezing (organized \+ easy)**
1. Stack rotis with parchment between each
2. Stack in **sets of 3–4 rotis**
3. Place in zip-lock or airtight box
4. Label with date

🧊 **Best use:** 2–3 weeks  
 🧊 **Safe up to:** 1 month

---
## **🔥 Daily reheat (quick reminder)**

* Reheat **directly from frozen** on hot tawa
* Cook till brown spots appear
* Wrap in cloth for 2–3 minutes
* Brush with ghee if packing

---
## **🧠 Pro batch-cook tips**
* If cooking takes long, keep unused dough balls **covered \+ lightly oiled**
* If last few rotis crack → knead in 1–2 tbsp hot water
* Slightly **thicker rotis freeze better** than paper-thin ones
# **🫓 Soft Jowar–Flax–Psyllium Roti (Gluten-Free)**

### **Makes: 6 medium rotis**
Texture goal: **soft, foldable, wheat-like**

---

## **🧾 Ingredients (measured precisely)**

* **Jowar flour** – 1 cup (120 g)

* **Flaxseed powder** – 2 tbsp (10 g)  
   *(grind fresh if possible)*

* **Psyllium husk (isabgol)** – 2 tsp (6 g)  
   👉 *Elasticity \+ softness*

* **Salt** – ½ tsp

* **Oil or ghee** – 2 tsp  
   👉 *Very important for softness*

* **Hot water** – \~¾ cup (180 ml)  
   👉 *Use hot, not warm*

**Optional but excellent for softness**

* **Curd (yogurt)** – 1 tbsp

---

## **🔥 Step-by-step method (follow in order)**

### **1️⃣ Mix dry ingredients**

In a wide bowl, combine:

* Jowar flour

* Flaxseed powder

* Psyllium husk

* Salt

Mix very well so psyllium is evenly distributed.

---

### **2️⃣ Add oil/ghee**

Add **2 tsp oil or ghee** and rub it into the flour with your fingers.

👉 **Softness reason:** fat coats starch → prevents drying later.

---

### **3️⃣ Add HOT water (critical)**

* Boil water and let it cool **30–40 seconds**

* Add gradually while mixing with a spoon

The dough will look sticky at first — that’s correct.

👉 **Softness reason:** hot water gelatinizes jowar starch.

---

### **4️⃣ First knead (gentle)**

* Once cool enough to touch, knead lightly

* Dough should be **soft, slightly tacky**, not stiff

If dry → add 1–2 tbsp hot water  
 If too sticky → rest, don’t add flour yet

---

### **5️⃣ Rest the dough (DO NOT SKIP)**

Cover and rest **10 minutes**.

👉 **Softness reason:** psyllium absorbs water and creates elasticity.

---

### **6️⃣ Second knead (important)**

After resting:

* Knead for **1–2 minutes**

* Dough should now feel **smooth, stretchy, crack-free**

If cracks appear → wet your fingers and knead again.

---

### **7️⃣ Divide & shape**

* Divide into **6 equal balls**

* Lightly grease palms with oil

* Roll balls smooth (no cracks)

---

### **8️⃣ Rolling method (very important)**

* Place dough ball between **two plastic sheets or parchment**

* Gently roll to **6–7 inch** circle

* Do **not** use dry flour for rolling

👉 **Dry flour \= hard roti**

---

## **🍳 Cooking (timing matters)**

### **9️⃣ Tawa heat**

* Heat tawa on **medium-high**

* It should be hot but not smoking

---

### **🔟 Cook the roti**

1. Place roti on hot tawa

2. Cook until color changes & light spots appear

3. Flip → cook other side

4. Press gently with cloth/spatula

5. Small brown spots appear → remove immediately

⛔ Do NOT overcook (main cause of hardness)

**Jowar (sorghum)** \= main flour base

**Flax seeds** \= healthy fats \+ protein

**Psyllium husk (isabgol)** \= binder \+ extra fiber (helps replace gluten)
