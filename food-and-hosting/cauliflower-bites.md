# Mexican-Inspired Cauliflower Bites

## Ingredients
- 1 head cauliflower (or frozen, thawed and well-drained)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp oregano
- ¼ tsp salt, black pepper to taste
- Juice of ½ lime (optional)
- Optional finish: Bitchin' Sauce or hot sauce

## Directions

1. Break into bite-size florets. If frozen, thaw and dry well.
2. Toss in olive oil and all spices. Add lime juice if using.

**Air Fryer** (preferred for crispiness):
400°F / 200°C for 15–18 minutes, shake halfway.

**Oven:**
425°F / 220°C, 25–30 minutes on parchment-lined sheet, flip halfway.

Finish with Bitchin' Sauce drizzle or lime juice. Garnish with cilantro.
