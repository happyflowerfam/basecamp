# High-Protein Banana Egg Mini Muffins (Lunchbox-Friendly)

Quick, soft mini muffins with no added sugar and plenty of protein. Great for kids and adults.

Makes 12 mini muffins

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas
- 3 eggs
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- **¼ tsp baking powder**
- **Pinch of baking soda (optional but helps balance yogurt)**
- **¼ tsp cinnamon**
- **1–2 Tbsp honey or maple syrup** (important!)
- Pinch of salt
- ½ cup Greek yogurt or cottage cheese  

**Optional add-ins**
- Mini chocolate chips  
- Chopped nuts  
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (add 1–2 Tbsp milk if batter thickens)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to **180°C / 350°F**. Grease a mini muffin tin.
2. In a bowl, whisk bananas, eggs, and yogurt until smooth.
3. Stir in flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.
4. Spoon batter into muffin cups (¾ full).
5. Bake **12–15 minutes**, until set and lightly golden.
6. Cool completely before packing.

## Notes
- APF makes these lighter and more cake-like than oat flour.
- If batter seems runny, add **1–2 Tbsp flour**.
- Freeze well for up to 2 months.

## Nutrition (approx.)
- 6–8g protein per mini muffin (varies with add-ins)
