# Vanilla Reduced-Sugar Cake

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup unsalted butter (softened)
- ½ cup granulated sugar (or ⅓ cup if you prefer less sweet)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¾ cup plain yogurt or unsweetened applesauce (for moisture)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour an 8-inch round or square pan.
2. Mix flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
3. Beat butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
4. Mix in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla extract.
5. Alternately add dry ingredients and yogurt/applesauce, starting and ending with dry.
6. Pour into pan and bake 25–30 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool 10 minutes in pan before transferring to a wire rack.

## Tips
- Add zest of 1 lemon or orange for extra flavor.
- Substitute half the sugar with honey or maple syrup.
- Top with unsweetened whipped cream or a light dusting of powdered sugar.
