# 🥥 Coconut Mushroom Noodle Bowl – Quick Checklist

### Ingredients (4 servings)
- 16 oz firm tofu  
- 1 cup chopped mushrooms  
- 1 cup coconut milk  
- 1 cup water  
- 1 tbsp peanut butter (optional)  
- 1 tsp lime juice or vinegar  
- 1–2 tsp Worcestershire  
- ½–¾ tsp salt  
- 1 cup green beans  
- 1–2 cups bok choy (stems separate)  
- Rice noodles  
- 2–3 tsp oil  
- 1½ tbsp cornstarch  

---

## Step 1 – Prep Tofu
- Drain & press 30–45 min  
- Cut 1–1.5" cubes, pat dry  

## Step 2 – Coat Tofu
- Toss with 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp Worcestershire (optional), rest 3–5 min  
- Sprinkle cornstarch (pinch baking powder optional), toss lightly  

## Step 3 – Air Fry Tofu (Ninja Flip)
- Preheat 375°F 3–5 min, lightly oil basket  
- Arrange tofu single layer, cook 9–10 min, flip  
- Cook 6–8 min more, cool → store in fridge  

## Step 4 – Sauce
- Sauté mushrooms 5–7 min  
- Add garlic 30 sec  
- Add coconut milk, water, Worcestershire, peanut butter → simmer 5 min  
- Add lime/vinegar + salt, cool → store  

## Step 5 – Vegetables
- Green beans: just shy of tender  
- Bok choy stems: 1–2 min before done  
- Bok choy leaves: last 30–60 sec  
- Store separately or in sauce  

## Step 6 – Noodles
- Cook 1 min less than package, drain & toss with tiny oil, store  

## Step 7 – Assembly (Later)
- Reheat sauce → add veg  
- Place noodles in bowls → pour sauce + veg  
- Reheat tofu 3–5 min → top bowls → serve  

---

## Tips
- Sauce thickens in fridge → add water if needed  
- Slightly undercooked noodles & veg prevent mushiness  
- Bigger tofu cubes → soft inside, crispy outside  
- Optional: chili, herbs, sesame seeds
