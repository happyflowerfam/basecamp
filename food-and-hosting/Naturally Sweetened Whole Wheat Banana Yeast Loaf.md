(Maximum Rise Version | 2 Bananas | 9" Loaf Pan)

Ingredients
- 2 very ripe bananas (about 1 cup / 240g mashed)
- 2 1/4 tsp (7g) active dry yeast
- 1/4 cup (60g) warm milk or water (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp (28g) oil or melted butter
- 2 cups (240g) whole wheat flour
- 1–2 tbsp additional milk or water (for softer dough)
- 1 tbsp vital wheat gluten (optional but recommended for height)
- 1 tsp salt

Target dough temperature: 78–80°F (26–27°C) during fermentation.

Method

1) Autolyse (improves rise significantly)  
Mix bananas, egg, oil, flour, vital wheat gluten (if using), and extra milk.  
Do NOT add yeast or salt yet.  
Rest 20–30 minutes at room temperature.

2) Add yeast and salt  
Bloom yeast in warm milk/water 5–10 minutes until foamy.  
Add to dough with salt. Mix thoroughly.

3) Knead  
Knead 10–12 minutes until smooth and elastic.  
Dough should be soft and slightly tacky, not stiff.  
Aim for dough temperature of 78–80°F.

4) Bulk rise  
Cover and ferment at 78–80°F until about 1.5x in size (not doubled).  
About 1.5–2 hours.  
Optional: after 45–60 minutes at room temp, refrigerate 8–16 hours (38–42°F) for better flavor and softer texture.

5) Shape  
Shape tightly and place in greased 9" loaf pan.  
Pan should be filled about 1/2 to 2/3 full.

6) Final proof  
Proof at 78–80°F until dough is puffy and just crowns the pan slightly.  
Do not overproof.  
A gentle poke should slowly spring back.  
If dough was refrigerated, allow it to warm before judging readiness.

7) Bake  
Preheat oven fully to 350°F (175°C).  
Optional: create steam for first 5–7 minutes (ice cubes in a preheated tray).  
Bake 35–45 minutes.  
Internal temperature should reach 190–195°F (88–90°C).

Cool completely before slicing.

Expected result: tallest possible rise with 100% whole red wheat, soft structured crumb, mild natural sweetness, excellent oven spring.
