# Sheet-Pan Salmon Dinner + Garlic Dough Balls  
**Cold kitchen (60°F) · One oven · T = 0 is dinner served**

---

## Garlic Dough Balls (Stone-Ground Red Wheat)

### Ingredients
**Dry**
- 1 cup Bob’s Red Mill stone-ground red wheat flour  
- 1¼ tsp active dry yeast  
- ¾ tsp salt  
- ½ tsp sugar or honey  

**Wet**
- ¾ cup warm milk or water (100–105°F)  
- 1½ tbsp olive oil or melted butter  

**Flavor**
- 1 small clove garlic, very finely grated  

---
### Dough Method (Earlier in the Day)
1. Wake Up the Yeast (10 min)
	- Mix warm liquid + sugar + yeast.
	- Rest 5–10 min until foamy.
2. Mix + Autolyse (30 min)
	- Add flour + garlic.
	- Stir until shaggy.
	- Cover and rest 30 min.
3. Add Salt + Fat, Knead (5–6 min)
	- Knead until soft and slightly tacky.
	- Add 1–2 tsp water if stiff.
---
### Oven Proofing Setup (Cold Kitchen Fix)
1. Turn oven on for **1–2 minutes**, then turn **off**.
2. Place a mug or small pan of hot water inside.
3. Oven should feel warm, not hot (≈75–85°F).
---
4. Bulk Rise (2½–3 hours)
	- Dough in lightly oiled bowl.
	- Covered, in warm (off) oven.
	- Dough should be puffy and relaxed (not necessarily doubled).
	- Optional stretch-and-fold halfway through.
5. Shape Dough Balls
	- Divide into golf-ball-sized pieces.
	- Tuck edges underneath for surface tension.
	- Place on parchment-lined sheet pan.
6. Final Proof (1½–2 hours)
	- Covered, back in warm (off) oven.
	- Dough balls should look pillowy.
	- Light poke springs back slowly.

➡️ Once fully proofed, remove from oven and keep covered at room temp until bake time.
---
## Salmon Marinade (6 hours max)

- 3 tbsp olive oil  
- 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari  
- 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey  
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard  
- 2 cloves garlic, grated  
- Zest + juice of ½ lemon  
- Black pepper  

Marinate salmon in fridge up to 6 hours.  
Before baking, wipe off excess marinade.
---
## Vegetables

### Sweet Potatoes
- 1-inch cubes  
- Olive oil, salt, pepper  
- Smoked paprika  
- Optional pinch chili flakes or cinnamon  

### Asparagus
- Olive oil  
- Salt, pepper  
- Optional lemon zest or garlic powder  
---
## Chili Oil Finish for Salmon (Make Ahead or Last-Minute)

- 2 tbsp chili oil  
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup  
- ½ tsp rice vinegar or lemon juice  

Whisk and set aside.
---
## FINAL OVEN SCHEDULE  
**425°F / 220°C · One oven · T = 0 dinner served**

### T – 55 min
- Dough balls uncovered, resting at room temp.
- Salmon, asparagus, sweet potatoes prepped.

### T – 50 min
- Preheat oven to **425°F**  
  (Confirm oven is fully hot after earlier proofing.)

### T – 40 min
- Sweet potatoes into oven (middle rack).

### T – 20 min
- Pull pan, flip sweet potatoes.
- Add salmon + asparagus to same pan.

### T – 20 min (same moment)
- Brush dough balls lightly with olive oil.
- Dough balls into oven **top rack**.

### T =0 min
- Remove dough balls → immediately brush with:
  - Melted butter
  - Fresh garlic
  - Pinch salt
  - Parsley or oregano

- Pull salmon when flaky and just cooked.
- Asparagus lightly charred, sweet potatoes caramelized.

---
## T = 0 (Dinner Served)
### Final Salmon Finish
- Drizzle chili oil lightly over salmon.
- Optional: flaky salt + extra lemon squeeze.
- Pinch of toasted sesame or a few drops toasted sesame oil (nutty)
---
## Notes
- Dough balls can rest baked and covered for 10–15 minutes if needed.
- Salmon should be pulled exactly when done — don’t wait on bread.
- Whole wheat dough values softness over dramatic rise.

Enjoy your extremely dialed-in dinner.
