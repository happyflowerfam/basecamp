# 🥗 Romaine, Orange & Pumpkin Seed Salad

## Ingredients
- 1 large head romaine lettuce, washed and chopped  
- 2 oranges, peeled and segmented  
- ½ cup pumpkin seeds (toasted)  
- ½ cucumber, sliced (optional)  
- 1 avocado, diced (optional)  
- Simple vinaigrette (olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper)  

## Instructions
1. Wash and chop romaine lettuce. Store wrapped in paper towel until serving.  
2. Toast pumpkin seeds in a dry pan until fragrant.  
3. Just before serving, toss lettuce with oranges, pumpkin seeds, and any extras (cucumber/avocado).  
4. Serve dressing on the side or lightly drizzle before serving.

# 🍢 Chicken or Tofu Satay with Peanut Sauce

## Ingredients
**Satay:**
- 900 g chicken thighs/breasts, cut into strips **OR** 400 g extra-firm tofu (pressed and cubed/striped)  
- 2 ½ tbsp soy sauce (or tamari)  
- 1 ¼ tbsp honey or brown sugar  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tbsp ginger, grated (or 1 tsp ground)  
- 1 ¼ tbsp curry powder or turmeric (optional)  
- 2 ½ tbsp oil  
- Skewers (soaked in water)  

**Curry powder**
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp paprika (sweet or smoked — optional, adds warmth)
- ½ tsp ground ginger (or fresh grated in the marinade)
- ¼ tsp cinnamon (optional, a subtle sweetness)
- Pinch of chili powder or cayenne (adjust to taste, optional)
- Lemongrass or lemon zest (optional)

**Peanut Sauce:**
- ½ cup peanut butter  
- 2 ½ tbsp soy sauce  
- 2 ½ tbsp lime juice  
- 1 ¼ tbsp honey  
- 1 ¼ tsp chili paste (optional)  
- 150 ml coconut milk (or water to thin)  

## Instructions
1. Marinate chicken or tofu with soy, honey, garlic, ginger, curry powder, and oil. Chill at least 30 min (up to overnight).  
2. Thread onto skewers.  
3. Cook chicken: grill or broil 3–4 min per side until cooked through.  
   For tofu: bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20–25 min or pan-fry until golden.  
4. Make peanut sauce: whisk all ingredients until smooth, thinning with coconut milk/water.  
5. Serve skewers with peanut sauce for dipping.

# 🦃 Gluten-Free Turkey Meatballs

## Ingredients
- 900 g ground turkey (not extra-lean)  
- 1 cup gluten-free breadcrumbs  
- ½ cup grated parmesan (optional)  
- 2 eggs  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)  
- ½ onion, finely grated (optional)  
- 2 tbsp parsley (fresh) or 1 tsp dried  
- 1 tsp salt  
- ½ tsp pepper  
- 1 tsp dried oregano (optional)  
- 2–3 tbsp milk or water  
- Olive oil for cooking  

## Instructions
1. Mix all ingredients gently in a large bowl until just combined.  
2. Shape into ~25–30 meatballs.  
3. Oven: Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 min, flipping halfway.  
   OR pan-fry until browned, then cook through.  
   OR simmer in sauce 15 min.  
4. Serve with spaghetti, sauce, or plain for kids.  
5. To freeze: bake first, cool, then freeze in a single layer.

# 🍫 Gluten-Free Chocolate Cake with Dates

## Ingredients
- 1 cup (130 g) gluten-free all-purpose flour blend (with xanthan gum)  
- ½ cup (50 g) cocoa powder  
- 1 tsp baking soda  
- ½ tsp baking powder  
- ½ tsp salt  
- ½ cup (100 g) sugar  
- ½ cup (120 g) date paste (blend soaked dates until smooth)  
- 2 eggs  
- ½ cup (120 ml) vegetable oil or melted butter  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- ¾ cup (180 ml) milk (reduce by 2–3 tbsp if using date paste)  
- ½ cup (120 ml) hot water or coffee  

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Line and grease a 9-inch round cake pan.  
2. Whisk flour, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.  
3. In another bowl, whisk sugar, date paste, eggs, oil, and vanilla. Stir in milk.  
4. Combine wet + dry, then stir in hot water/coffee. Batter will be thin.  
5. Bake 30–35 min until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely before icing.

# 🍫 Brown Sugar Chocolate Fudge Icing

## Ingredients
- ½ cup (115 g) butter (or dairy-free spread)  
- 1 cup (200 g) brown sugar  
- ¼ cup (60 ml) milk (dairy or non-dairy)  
- ½ cup (50 g) cocoa powder  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- Pinch of salt (optional)  

## Instructions
1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add brown sugar, stir until dissolved and glossy (2–3 min).  
2. Whisk in cocoa powder until smooth.  
3. Slowly whisk in milk, bring to a gentle bubble for 1 min.  
4. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla (and salt).  
5. Cool 5–10 min until slightly thickened, then spread over cooled cake.  
6. Sets into a glossy, fudgy finish.
