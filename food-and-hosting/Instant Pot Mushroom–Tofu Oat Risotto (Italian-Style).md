

Creamy, savory risotto vibes using steel-cut oats. Hands-off, delay-start friendly, and weeknight-proof.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup steel-cut oats
- 2½ cups water or vegetable broth
- 1–1½ cups mushrooms, sliced
- ½–1 cup firm tofu, cubed (large pieces)
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped or smashed
- ½ tsp salt (adjust later)
- Freshly ground black pepper
- ½ tsp thyme or Italian seasoning (optional)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter

**After cooking:**
- ½–¾ cup blanched frozen spinach, defrosted and well-squeezed
- Butter or olive oil, to finish
- Parmesan, pecorino, or nutritional yeast
- Lemon zest or small squeeze of lemon

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients **except spinach and finishing items** to the Instant Pot.
2. Stir once to combine.
3. Cook on **High Pressure for 10 minutes**.
4. Allow **Natural Release for 5 minutes**, then vent.
5. Open lid and **stir vigorously** to develop creaminess.
6. Stir in squeezed spinach. Let warm on *Keep Warm* for 2–3 minutes.
7. Finish with butter/olive oil, cheese or nutritional yeast, black pepper, and lemon.

## Notes
- For a looser risotto, stir in a splash of hot water or broth at the end.
- Oats will thicken as they sit—loosen before serving if needed.
- Optional umami boost: ½ tsp miso or soy sauce at the finish.

## Serving
Serve hot with extra black pepper and olive oil. Excellent eaten immediately or reheated with added liquid.
