# Kerala Meal Plan
Jul 14, 2023

## Menu

**Appetiser**
- Onion Paniyaram
- Coconut Chutney

**Main Course**
- Pulisseri
- Thoran
- Steamed Rice
- Rasam South
- Yogurt

**Accompaniments**
- Pickle
- Papad
- Yogurt

**Dessert**
- Rice vermicelli payasam

For Samā: millets, pulisseri, kheer, 1 round of paniyaram

## Cooking plan
- Cook pulisseri
- Cook rice vermicelli payasam
- Cook rice
- Cook rasam or South-Indian palak dal
- Make pappadum
- Just before they arrive: cook paniyaram
