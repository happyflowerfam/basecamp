### Brussels sprouts (air fryer)
If using fresh: boil 3–5 cups water, cook 4–5 min, drain.
Toss with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, maple syrup, red pepper flakes, garlic powder, salt, pepper, parmesan.
Air fry at 375°F for 12 min, shake halfway.

### Pomelo garlic chicken
Marinade: pomelo zest + slices, 1–2 tsp vinegar, honey, 3 tbsp olive oil, 4 garlic cloves, ¼ cup lemon juice, 2 tbsp fresh herbs, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper.
Marinate up to 4 hours.
Cook in hot skillet 3–4 min per side until golden. Add reserved marinade, boil, simmer until reduced. Return chicken to coat, heat 1 min. Rest 5 min. Serve with pomelo wedges.

### Beetroot-apple salad
Beetroot, apple, onion, feta, chickpeas, parsley.
Dressing: olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, sugar. Let flavors blend 20 min before serving.
