Go with the **8-inch cast iron skillet** 👍  
For **2 adults**, 5 eggs + a full mozzarella log will be **luxuriously thick and satisfying** in the 8". The 10" would spread it too thin and make it drier.

Below is the **final, dialed-in version** for exactly your setup.

---
## Ultra-Cheesy Sweet Potato & Mozzarella Skillet Bake

**Serves:** 2 hungry adults  
**Pan:** 8" cast iron  
**Style:** cheesy > eggy, sliceable but luscious

---

### Ingredients (no changes needed)
- **5 eggs**
- **1 full log mozzarella**, sliced thick or torn 
- 1–1½ **sweet potatoes**, small dice
- 1 **red onion**, thinly sliced
- Handful **spinach**
- Small amount **zucchini**, minced
- Fresh **basil**
- Olive oil
- Salt & black pepper

---

### Step 1: Roast sweet potatoes
1. Heat oven to **425°F / 220°C**.
2. Toss diced sweet potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper.
3. Roast **18–22 minutes** until tender and lightly caramelized.
4. Reduce oven to **375°F / 190°C**.

---

### Step 2: Build flavor in the skillet
1. Heat your **8" cast iron** over medium heat with olive oil.
2. Sauté red onion until soft and lightly golden.
3. Add zucchini → cook 1–2 min. 
4. Add spinach → wilt.
5. Turn off heat.

---

### Step 3: Assemble (important order)

1. Spread roasted sweet potatoes evenly over the vegetables.
2. Add **½ the mozzarella**, spacing it out.
3. Whisk **5 eggs** with salt & pepper.
4. Pour eggs evenly into the skillet.
5. Gently nudge with a fork so egg settles.
6. Top with **remaining mozzarella**, fully covering the surface.

_It will look like too much cheese. It is not._

---

### Step 4: Bake
- Bake at **375°F / 190°C** for **30–35 minutes**
- Center should jiggle slightly but not look wet
- Edges will be bubbling and golden

**Optional (recommended):**  
Broil **1–2 minutes** until the top blisters and browns.

---

### Step 5: Rest & serve
- Rest **10 minutes** (this lets it set)
- Finish with torn **fresh basil** and black pepper
- Slice into **4 thick wedges**

---

### What to expect on the plate

- Stretchy mozzarella pull    
- Creamy center, crisp edges from the cast iron
- Sweet potatoes give it substance so it pairs beautifully with tomato soup
- Focaccia for scooping molten cheese = correct choice
