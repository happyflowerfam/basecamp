# Basecamp

This is the household's shared operating system — travel, home, kids, finances, and life together.
It is co-owned and should be legible to both partners.

## Zones
- `home/` — home projects and decor
- `food-and-hosting/` — recipes, meal planning, and entertaining
- `sama/` — child's activities, school, and logistics
- `together/` — shared goals and date nights
- `travel/` — trip plans and itineraries
- `yard/` — yard and garden
- `finances/` — shared money, access, and affairs in order
- `sagas/` — closed chapters kept for reference

## How to help
**Both partners may use this.** Keep suggestions practical and neutral.
**Flag when something needs joint decision-making** vs. one person can just act.
**Be realistic about logistics** — time, cost, coordination required.

## Tone
- Direct and practical.
- No assumptions about who is doing what.
