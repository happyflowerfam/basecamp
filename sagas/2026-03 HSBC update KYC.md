# 🎯 action: HSBC update KYC

## 🧭 quick guide

What you need to do:
Please upload a scanned copy of one of the following documents, showing your current overseas address, via the HSBC India Mobile Banking App (dated within the last 3 months):
•	Passport
•	Driving License
•	OCI/PIO
•	Utility bill (electricity, water, gas)
•	Bank statement/Bank Reference letter
•	Registered rent agreement
Updating your address is quick and easy on the HSBC India Mobile Banking App!
•	Open the HSBC India mobile app and log in to your account
•	Go to Profile > Contact details > Addresses > Residential address > Upload the scanned document
•	Tap Edit and enter your current overseas residential address
•	Review and confirm the changes
