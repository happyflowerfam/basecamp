# Yard Design — Foster City

## Site overview
- **Location:** Foster City, CA — cool, windy bay-side microclimate; occasional summer heat spikes; mild winters
- **Soil:** Heavy clay (bay fill) throughout — compacts easily, drains slowly
- **Annual rainfall:** ~15–18", October–April
- **Size:** ~2,000 sq ft total

## Goals and constraints
- Natural, low-input approach throughout
- No pesticides as default — chemical intervention permitted as one-season exception for intractable problems (pennywort, woody resprouters)
- Less water than a conventional yard — cycle-and-soak irrigation for clay soil
- Safe for kids and family use
- Fall is the key action season for lawn and beds

## Irrigation
- Installed irrigation system throughout (except Bed 2 and cherry plum fence line)
- Method: cycle-and-soak — 2–3 short cycles per zone with 30–45 min breaks to manage clay absorption
- Summer target: 0.75–1" per week for lawn (less than inland areas due to Foster City's cool climate)
- Turn off controller when rain is regular (October–April)

### Cherry plum fence line
- **Location:** Fence line
- **Shade:** —
- **Irrigation:** Yes (established trees)
- **Plants:** 10 cherry plum trees
- **Notes:** Windbreak along bay-side fence; roots send suckers into back lawn seasonally — mow to manage
- **Pruning:** Prune late summer to early fall (August–September) for size control — limits regrowth response. Spring pruning after bloom encourages vigorous regrowth and is not recommended if goal is keeping trees smaller. Budget ~$2,000 for professional pruning of all 10 trees. Can split over two years (5 trees per year) to spread cost.
