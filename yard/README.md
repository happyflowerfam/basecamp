# Foster City Yard — Project Overview

## About this project
A ~2,000 sq ft yard in Foster City, CA being managed with a natural, low-input approach. Heavy clay (bay fill) soil throughout. Goals: green lawn through summer, no pesticides, safe for kids and family, less water than a conventional yard.

**Current phase:** Spring 2026 — active weed management, preparing for fall renovation

## Key decisions made
- **Grass:** transitioning to RTF + microclover via fall overseeding — do not strip and restart
- **Mowing:** always 3" — critical for RTF health, weed suppression, and play use
- **Irrigation:** cycle-and-soak method for clay soil; upgrading to Rachio 3 smart controller
- **Weeds:** Fiesta (iron-based) for pennywort and wild lettuce this season; Garlon 4 for woody resprouters (Norway maple, pomegranate, avocado)
- **Fall is the key action season** — aerate, topdress, overseed lawn; plant Bed 2 additions; prune cherry plums
- **Tools:** consolidating on EGO 56V battery platform
