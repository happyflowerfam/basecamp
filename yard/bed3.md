# Bed 3 — Kitchen Garden

## Conditions
- **Location:** TBD
- **Sun:** Partial shade from cherry plum trees and house
- **Water:** Irrigated
- **Soil:** Heavy clay (bay fill) — same as rest of yard
- **Goal:** Kitchen garden
- **Challenge:** Roly polies (pill bugs) — moist, shaded clay conditions favour them; damage seedlings and young transplants

## Existing plants

| Plant            | Type              | Keep/Remove | Notes                                                                                                         |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lemon tree**   | Fruiting tree     | Keep        | Worth keeping — productive in Foster City's mild climate                                                      |
| **Avocado**      | Fruiting tree     | Remove      | Large, established, deep roots — very hard to remove. Cut and paint with Garlon 4 (triclopyr); multiple treatments likely needed over several seasons |
| **Strawberries** | Fruiting groundcover | Keep     | Tolerate partial shade; already established — work well in a kitchen garden                                   |
| **Potatoes**     | Vegetable         | Keep/manage | Volunteers likely — harvest what's there; replant intentionally in spring                                     |

## Recommended kitchen garden plants

Suited to partial shade and Foster City's cool bay climate.

| Plant           | Type        | Season         | Notes                                                                    |
| --------------- | ----------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Kale**        | Leafy green | Year round     | Thrives in partial shade; very productive in Foster City's cool climate  |
| **Chard**       | Leafy green | Year round     | Tolerates shade well; low maintenance                                    |
| **Spinach**     | Leafy green | Fall–spring    | Prefers cool and partial shade — ideal here                              |
| **Lettuce**     | Leafy green | Fall–spring    | Bolts in full sun; partial shade extends the season                      |
| **Chives**      | Herb        | Year round     | Low maintenance, tolerates shade, perennial                              |
| **Parsley**     | Herb        | Year round     | Handles partial shade well                                               |
| **Cilantro**    | Herb        | Fall–spring    | Prefers cool conditions; bolts in heat — partial shade helps             |
| **Mint**        | Herb        | Year round     | Must be contained in a pot — spreads aggressively                        |

**Avoid in partial shade:** tomatoes, peppers, squash, cucumbers — need full sun to produce.

## Roly poly management

- Use transplants rather than direct seeding — established plants survive pill bug attention
- Sprinkle diatomaceous earth around seedling bases — safe, reapply after rain
- Clear debris, wood, and dense mulch where roly polies shelter
- Let bed dry out between waterings — they retreat when dry
- Problem reduces significantly once plants are established

## Key issues

- **Avocado removal:** deep-rooted and persistent — Garlon 4 cut-and-paint is the lowest labour option but may take several seasons. Kitchen garden conversion depends on resolving this.
- **Shade:** limits to leafy greens and herbs unless avocado canopy is removed
- **Roly polies:** manageable with transplants and diatomaceous earth; worse in spring when seedlings are tender
