# Bed 2 — Left of house

## Conditions
- **Location:** Left of house, along property
- **Sun:** Full sun, no shade — gets very hot
- **Water:** Not irrigated
- **Soil:** Heavy clay (bay fill) — same as rest of yard
- **Note:** Extreme summer heat limits planting options; drought-tolerant succulents and established shrubs are the best fit

## Plants

| Plant                            | Type             | Water needs | Notes                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Crassula jade**                | Succulent        | Low         | Well suited to unirrigated bed — drought tolerant                                                            |
| **Rambling aloe**                | Succulent        | Low         | Well suited to unirrigated bed — drought tolerant                                                            |
| **Hibiscus**                     | Flowering shrub  | Moderate    | Will struggle without irrigation in summer — hand water occasionally or move to Bed 1 (irrigated)           |
| **Variegated Japanese euonymus** | Shrub            | Low–Moderate | Tolerates dry conditions once established; full sun may bleach variegation slightly                         |
| **Mint**                         | Herb             | Moderate    | In enclosed container — dies back in summer heat, resprouts reliably. Containment handles spreading          |

## Recommended additions

Plants that thrive in hot, dry, unirrigated full sun — look good through summer without irrigation. **Plant in fall** — establishes in cool season before summer heat arrives.

### Favourites
| Plant                          | Type                    | Water needs | Notes                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lavender**                   | Mediterranean shrub     | Low         | Thrives in hot dry conditions; looks and smells great in summer; pollinators love it           |
| **Rosemary**                   | Mediterranean herb/shrub | Low        | Same conditions as lavender; doubles as kitchen herb; very low water once established          |
| **Salvia leucantha** (Mexican bush sage) | California native | Low  | Purple spikes in summer and fall; tough and drought tolerant; pollinators love it              |
| **Achillea** (yarrow)          | California native       | Low         | Feathery foliage, flowers all summer; very tough in dry clay; spreads gently                   |

### Also suitable
| Plant                | Type            | Water needs | Notes                                                                        |
| -------------------- | --------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agave**            | Succulent       | Low         | Architectural, virtually indestructible, looks good year round               |
| **Aloe vera**        | Succulent       | Low         | Useful, drought tolerant, handles reflected heat well                        |
| **Sedum**            | Succulent       | Low         | Low growing, spreads nicely, stays tidy                                      |
| **Cistus** (rockrose) | Mediterranean shrub | Low    | Beautiful summer flowers; very low water once established                    |
| **Festuca glauca** (blue fescue) | Ornamental grass | Low | Looks good year round; no irrigation needed once established           |

## Weeds to remove

| Plant            | Action | Notes                                                          |
| ---------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stray potato** | Pull   | Volunteer — pulls out easily in moist soil                     |
| **Mallow**       | Leave  | Will die back in summer heat; only pull if flowering           |

## Key issues

- **Hibiscus** is mismatched to a hot, unirrigated bed — monitor for summer stress
- **Mint** well managed in enclosure; dies back in summer and resprouts — no action needed
