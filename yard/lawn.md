# Eco-Lawn Plan — Foster City, CA

See [yard-design.md](yard-design.md) for full site conditions, goals, and irrigation setup. 

---

## Recommended base mix

**Rhizomatous Tall Fescue (RTF) + microclover (5–10% mix)**

RTF is the right grass for Foster City. It self-repairs bare patches via rhizomes — important for a lawn kids use — and its deep roots handle both clay soil and occasional heat spikes better than standard tall fescue. Foster City's cool bay climate is actually ideal for it: lower summer stress means it stays green with less water than you'd need in warmer inland areas. Microclover fixes nitrogen naturally, reducing fertilizer needs, and stays green even when grass is mildly stressed.

> If the lawn is currently standard tall fescue, overseed with RTF in fall. No need to strip and restart.

---

## Approach

- Transition gradually via fall overseeding with RTF + microclover
- Manage clay soil through annual aeration and compost topdressing
- Water using cycle-and-soak method suited to clay
- Mow consistently at 3" — right height for play and drought resilience
- Tolerate clover and low groundcovers; remove problem weeds by hand before they seed

## Seasonal maintenance

### Spring (now)
- Mow at 3" — keep consistent; Foster City's cool spring means strong growth through May
- Knock down seed heads weekly during peak flush
- Work through weed guide in [lawn-weeding.md](lawn-weeding.md); focus on removing before seed set
- Do not reseed in spring — wait for fall
- No irrigation needed — rainfall handles it; turn off controller if rains are regular
- **Optional:** Apply gypite or agricultural gypsum (~25 lbs per 1,000 sq ft) to begin loosening clay structure — natural, not a pesticide, safe for kids

### Summer
- **Target:** 0.75–1" of water per week — less than a conventional lawn, achievable given Foster City's cool climate
- **Use cycle-and-soak** due to clay: run each irrigation zone in 2–3 short cycles (6–8 min each) with 30–45 min breaks between, to let water absorb rather than run off
- Water 2x per week, early morning (less wind, less evaporation; avoids fungal issues in cool nights)
- During occasional heat spikes (90°F+), add one extra watering cycle — clay holds heat and roots stress fast without moisture
- Mow at 3" every 1–2 weeks; never cut more than 1/3 of blade height at once
- Leave clippings on lawn — free nitrogen
- Expect the lawn to look good now: RTF + clover holds color well in Foster City summers

### Fall (key step)
- **Aerate first** — critical given clay soil and kid foot traffic; consider aerating twice yearly (fall mandatory, spring optional) for heavy clay
- Top dress lightly with compost (1/4") and work into aeration holes — improves clay structure over time
- Overseed with RTF + microclover blend (~7 lbs per 1,000 sq ft for overseeding = ~14 lbs total)
- Water to establish: run irrigation 2x daily, short cycles, for 2–3 weeks
- Taper to normal schedule once seed germinates
- Turn irrigation controller to rain/auto-off as fall rains arrive

### Winter
- Minimal work; growth slows but Foster City stays mild enough for occasional mowing
- Turn off irrigation — rain handles it
- Note bare or thin spots for fall overseeding priority areas

## Seasonal look

- **Spring:** Lush, actively growing, mixed green — mowing keeps it tidy and even for play
- **Summer:** Dense, even green with consistent watering; RTF holds color better than standard fescue; clover patches slightly brighter; cool Foster City climate means less summer stress than inland areas
- **Fall:** Lawn recovers quickly as fog and rain return; overseeded areas germinate and blend in within 3–4 weeks
- **Winter:** Mostly green, slower growth, low maintenance

## Irrigation system setup (summer schedule)

For a 2,000 sq ft lawn with clay soil in Foster City:

- **Frequency:** 2x per week (e.g., Tuesday and Friday), early morning (5–7am)
- **Method:** Cycle and soak — program each zone to run 3 cycles of 6–8 min with 30–45 min between cycles
- **Total per zone per session:** ~18–24 min (in cycles, not continuous)
- **Adjust by season:** Use controller's seasonal adjustment feature — 40–50% of summer output in spring/fall, off in winter
- **Watch for:** Pooling or runoff (reduce cycle time); dry patches near fence lines (wind shadow — may need a head adjustment or hand watering)

## End result

- A durable, natural lawn that handles kids and casual play year-round
- Green through summer with moderate water — less than a conventional Foster City lawn
- Self-repairing from foot traffic via RTF rhizomes
- No pesticides — managed through mowing, competition, and hand weeding
- Clay soil improves in structure and drainage each year with aeration and compost topdressing
