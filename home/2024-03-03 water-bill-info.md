# Foster City Water Bill Info

**Start/stop service:** Turn-ons scheduled business days 8am–3pm. Be present when water is turned back on (need inside access to check faucets).

**Stop service:** Complete [Termination Service Form](https://www.fostercity.org/finance/page/water-and-wastewater-termination-form). Must be a business day; cannot be backdated. Final bill within 3 weeks (can request calculation within 2 business days).

**Billing cycle:** ~61-day cycle (bill every other month). Irrigation meters and accounts started after Feb 2020 billed monthly. Due date printed on bill.

**Pay:** City Hall (cash/check) · Online: [onlinebiller.com/fostercity](https://www.onlinebiller.com/fostercity) — free, 24/7, accepts e-check, Visa, MC (3% fee for card). Need account number + service address to sign up.

**Restore after non-payment disconnect:** Pay delinquent amount + turn-on charge. After hours: call 650-286-3345.

**Property owner responsibility:** Unpaid charges remain with property owner per Ordinance No. 126/Section 8.44.020.
