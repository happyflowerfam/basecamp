## Bear Engineering — Nathan's assessment (Feb 10, 2024)
- Movement: 2.9–0.4 inches. Not minor, not the worst. Better than Foster City average.
- Clay soil: pushes water out over ~30 years, tends to stabilize. Expansive soil keeps moving.

## Drainage and sequence guidance (Feb 16, 2024)
- Drainage design: consult Geoforensics (Dan) or a geotech/civil engineer — not a contractor.
- Foster City = flat neighborhood, naturally high water table. French drain ineffective here.
- Water comes up from underneath; sump pump is mechanical/electrical (failure points) — size carefully.
- Vapor barrier = layer between crawl space ground and living space (reduces mustiness).
- Crawl space encapsulation = all seams sealed/taped — more complete solution.
- Sequence: foundation repair → seismic retrofit → dig for sump pump.
- Earthquake retrofit: protects occupants (limits damage); earthquake insurance: protects financial investment. Get separate costing.
