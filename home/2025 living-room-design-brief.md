## Room dimensions
- Length: 240 inches (20 ft)
- Width: 173 inches (~14.4 ft)

## Architectural features
- **Top wall (173"):** Full, uninterrupted wall
- **Window wall (right, 240"):** Low window, 117" wide × 19" high, centered
- **Dining room entrance (left, 240"):** 114" wide opening, toward the top end
- **Entryway:** Bottom wall
- **Cove:** Small reading-nook-sized space between entryway and dining room walls

## Furniture specs
- Sofa: 34"H × 88"W × 38"D

## Design principles
- No TV as focal point — prioritize conversation
- Conversational distance throughout
- Walkway: 30" wide from entryway to dining room
- Leave 2–8" gap between sofa and walls (visual breathing room)
- Exploring sofa on top wall

- [ ] Take measurements and draw a plan
