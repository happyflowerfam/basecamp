# Stone Care

## Granite (steps)
1. **Clean** — use granite cleaner for natural stone; paper towel or lint-free cloth
2. **Seal** — spray sealer, wipe immediately with lint-free cloth, buff dry. Repeat 2–3×, wait 20 min between coats. Use after 2 hours.
3. **Polish** — wait 24 hours after sealing. Spray polish, buff, wipe dry. Do not polish stone floors.
